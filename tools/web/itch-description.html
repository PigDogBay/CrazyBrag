Pull up a chair, stranger. Crazy Brag is a quick-fire game of three card brag, played for lives in a dusty saloon town against a table of card sharps: Chris, Howie, Pig Dog, Bomber and Geordie.

Everyone starts with three lives. Each round you get one move to better your hand, then the cards are turned over and the worst hand at the table loses a life. Lose all three and you are out. The last player standing wins the game.

There are two ways to play:

  • Deadwood – the straight game, easy to pick up.
  • Looky Looky – the house rules: jokers, wild twos, extra lives, and three sneaky actions you can take before your move.

Choose your game with the GAME button on the title screen, press DEAL, and good luck. The HELP button has the rules in the game too.

Win games to rise through the ranks, from Cowpoke and Buckaroo all the way to Card King.

How to play your turn: tap or click a card in your hand, then a card in the middle to swap them. Or tap all three of your cards to swap your whole hand for the middle.

Plays in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone. Sound starts on your first tap.

Deadwood Rules

The deal. Every player is dealt three cards, face down, and three more go in the middle of the table: two face up and one face down.

Your turn. Starting to the dealer's left, each player takes one turn. You must do one of two things:

  • Swap one card from your hand with one card from the middle. You may take the face-down card, but you won't know what it is until you have it.
  • Swap all three of your cards for the three in the middle. The first card you pick from your hand goes into the middle face down.

The showdown. When everyone has played (the dealer goes last), all hands are shown. The lowest hand loses a life. If two or more players tie for the lowest hand, they all lose a life.

Out of the game. A player with no lives left is out. Play carries on until only one player remains.

The hands, from lowest to highest:

  1. High card – nothing better, so your highest card counts. Aces are high. 5-3-2 is the lowest hand there is.
  2. Pair – two cards of the same rank.
  3. Flush – all three cards of the same suit.
  4. Run – three ranks in a row, in any suits. A-2-3 is the highest run, then Q-K-A, J-Q-K, and so on down to 2-3-4.
  5. Trotter – a run in one suit.
  6. Prial – three of a kind. Threes are the highest prial, then aces, kings, and so on down to twos.

Prial out, everyone out! If the best hand at the showdown is a prial, every other player loses a life, not just the lowest.

Looky Looky Rules

Looky Looky is Deadwood with a few house rules. Everything above still applies, plus:

Extra Life. The first time you win a round with a prial you gain a life, and nobody loses one that round. The grey heart next to your lives is the extra life waiting to be won. After that, your prials work as usual.

Bullet Proof. When a prial wins the round, any player holding a trotter or a lesser prial is safe and keeps their life.

Jokers. Two jokers join the pack:

  • Wild Bill is wild. He plays as whichever card makes your hand best, as long as it is not a card you already hold.
  • Calamity Jane is a dud. She counts for nothing and is the lowest card in the pack.

Deuces Are Wild. A two can be the two of any suit, though it is always a two. So 2♦ K♠ 9♠ is a king flush, and 2♥ 3♠ 4♠ is a trotter.

Actions. Before you make your move you may take up to two of these three actions, once each per turn, using the buttons next to your hand:

  • Looky Looky – peek at the face-down card in the middle. Only you get to see it.
  • Stash Hand – throw in all three of your cards and be dealt three new ones.
  • Change a Card – pick one card in the middle to be replaced with a new one from the pack.

The other players can do the same, so watch what they are up to.

The marked deck. Look closely at the backs of the cards. In Howie's deck every card is marked, and a player with sharp eyes and a good memory can learn to read them. If you would rather play it straight, switch to the plain deck with the GAME button.
